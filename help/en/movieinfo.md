# Movie Info

The middle shows the data of the movie you selected in the movie list. You can change every field. As soon as something differs from the file, the movie gets a yellow dot in the list.

## Fields

- Type **Title**, **Description**, **Release Date** and **Studio** directly.
- Choose the **Genre** from the list or type your own. The list comes from TMDB, in the language of the movie data (see **Settings**).
- **Actors**, **Directors**, **Producers** and **Screenplay** consist of single names, see below.

## Names

Type a name and press Enter. Separate several names with a comma to enter them at once. A name that is already there is not added twice.

![Names as a list, the × appears on the name under the mouse](:/images/help/actors.png)

- **Remove:** Rest the mouse on a name and an **×** appears. Or click the name and press Delete or Backspace.
- **Order:** Drag a name to its new place.
- **Unselect:** Escape, a click beside it, or Tab.

## Cover

- **New cover:** Drag an image from your file manager or your browser onto the cover.
- **Drag the cover out:** Drag the cover into your file manager or another app; it arrives as a JPG image.
- **Remove the cover:** Click the **×** at the top right of the cover (it appears when the mouse is over the cover). Or click the cover and press Delete or Backspace. Unselect: Escape, a click beside it, or Tab.

Below the cover you see the size of the video, for example **1080p**, with the exact size in pixels beside it. Saving sets the file's HD flag from it.

## Age Rating

The system already in the file decides which steps the list shows – a movie with a German FSK rating shows the FSK steps, even if you chose another country in the settings. The system in the settings only applies to movies that have no age rating yet.

If MovieMetaM doesn't know the system or the step in the file, it is kept: the list shows an extra entry "System: step", and the movie does not count as changed.

## Content Advisory

**None**, **Clean** or **Explicit** – Apple TV and iTunes, for example, use it to mark movies with offensive content.
