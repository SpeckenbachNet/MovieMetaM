# MovieMetaM – Changelog

## 0.9 – Mac version (October 22, 2026)

- First finished Mac version as a DMG, runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs
- Version 0.9 because the translations other than German and English have not been checked by native speakers yet

## 0.8 – Help, languages, polish (October 21–22, 2026)

- Built-in help for all areas, with screenshots
- User interface also in French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese and Korean (drafts), the help also in French and Spanish
- Own genres, the setting "Only fill empty fields" and an own TMDB token
- Remove the cover, drop whole folders onto the movie list
- Files that cannot be read are recognized and not added
- Follows the switch between light and dark mode while running

## 0.7 – Handling and safety (October 20–21, 2026)

- Remove names (cast, directors …) with × and reorder them by dragging
- Warning about unsaved changes when closing and removing
- All icons drawn in-house

## 0.6 – Saving (October 20, 2026)

- The data is written into the MP4 file, optionally with "safe saving" via a copy
- Content advisory and video format (e.g. 1080p) are shown

## 0.5 – Applying movie data (October 20, 2026)

- Apply results from TMDB with all details, choose a poster, apply the age rating

## 0.4 – Age ratings and languages (October 19–20, 2026)

- Age ratings from 46 countries with their badges
- Separate languages for the app and for the movie data
- Messages appear briefly on screen instead of in a status bar

## 0.3 – New look (October 17–19, 2026)

- Rounded design throughout in the system colors, light and dark
- Search results with posters and an enlarged preview
- Window and column widths are remembered

## 0.1 – 0.2 – Beginnings (until September 2026)

- Reading the metadata from MP4 files, movie search on TMDB, first user interface
